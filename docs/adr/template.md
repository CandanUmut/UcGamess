## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints are real, and where did they
come from? Include the numbers and the sources — the reasoning is only useful
later if it can be checked.

## Decision

What we are doing. State it plainly, in one or two sentences.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. "It was worse" is not a
reason; "its runtime is 15–25 MB and download size maps to conversion" is.

## Consequences

**Good** — what this buys us.

**Bad** — what it costs, and what we are accepting.

**Reversibility** — how expensive is it to change our mind? What would we have
to redo?

## Revisit if

Concrete conditions that would make this decision wrong. If you cannot name any,
the decision is probably not worth an ADR.
