## Title

Beeline

## Short description (one line, ≤ 120 characters)

Build a branching flight network from the hive on a budget of wax, and send
your swarm to the richest flowers before sunset.

## Description

Every bee in the hive is waiting for you to point the way — and every line
you draw costs wax.

Drag a line from the hive to a flower and a crew of bees flies it and brings
honey home. Drag from any line to branch it: a branch only pays for the new
part. The richest flowers bloom far away in clusters, and there is never
enough wax to reach everything with separate lines. Build one trunk out there
and branch from it, and the meadow is yours.

- **30 levels in three worlds.** Spring Meadow teaches the network, Bramble
  Maze hides the good flowers behind hedges, and Wasp Summer sends raiders
  after what you built.
- **Every star is a better plan.** Three stars means your network was good,
  not just your reflexes. Fill the honeycomb map cell by cell; new worlds open
  as your stars add up.
- **Nothing is wasted.** A flower runs dry? Carry its line on to the next one,
  branch off it, or hold it to take all its wax back.
- **Keep the hive busy.** Keep every bee flying and the multiplier climbs to
  ×5.
- **Golden blooms** open for a few seconds and pay six a trip — branch to them
  fast.
- **Endless mode**: how many days can your hive last?

No sign-up, no downloads. Progress saves automatically.

## Controls

| Action                     | Mouse                            | Touch                    |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| Send bees to a flower      | Drag from the hive to the flower | Drag from the hive       |
| Branch a line              | Drag from anywhere on a line     | Same                     |
| Route around a hedge       | Drag on from the end of a line   | Same                     |
| Swat a wasp                | Click it                         | Tap it                   |
| Take a line back (refund)  | Press and hold on the line       | Press and hold           |
| Pause, retry, sound on/off | Pause button (top right) or `P`  | Pause button (top right) |

## Technical

- HTML5 (Phaser), 16:9, scales to any window; landscape on phones (portrait
  shows a "turn your phone" prompt).
- Works with mouse, touch and keyboard (`P` pauses).
- About 1 MB download (under 1 MB compressed on first load).
- Saves progress through the portal's save system, or in the browser elsewhere.
- Sound: its own synthesized effects plus one CC0 music track; mutes during ads.
- Age rating: suitable for everyone (PEGI 3 content; no violence beyond
  cartoon wasp-swatting, no chat, no purchases).
